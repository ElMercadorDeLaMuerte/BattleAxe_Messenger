# BattleAxe_Messenger
BattleAxe is a secure, end-to-end encrypted messaging application built on a zero-knowledge architecture. It is designed for users who require absolute privacy and uncompromising security. There are no compromises, no recovery keys, and no backdoors.

⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING: NO PASSWORD RECOVERY
BattleAxe operates on a strict zero-knowledge principle. We do not store your password, nor do we have the ability to reset it. If you forget your password, your account and all associated messages are gone forever. There is no "Forgot Password" link, no email recovery, and no support override. Memorize your credentials or use a secure password manager.

Core Features
Absolute Encryption: Every message, file, and call is end-to-end encrypted. Only you and the intended recipient possess the keys to decrypt the data.

Zero Backdoors: Our architecture mathematically guarantees that no third party—including the BattleAxe development team, governments, or ISPs—can intercept or read your communications.

Zero-Knowledge Infrastructure: We do not log your metadata, your contacts, or your chat history. The servers merely act as blind relays to deliver encrypted packets.
